FREQUENTLY ASKED

COMMON QUESTIONS

What mount system do you use?

All Lenscraft cameras and lenses use the E-mount system with full electronic communication for autofocus, stabilisation, and metadata.

Do lenses work with other camera brands?

Lenscraft lenses are natively E-mount compatible. Third-party adapters may allow use on other systems with varying functionality.

What is the warranty period?

All Lenscraft products carry a 3-year manufacturer warranty. Extended coverage options are available at purchase.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Yes — students, educators, and accredited film schools receive 15–25% discounts. Contact us with your institutional details.
