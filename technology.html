SENSOR ARCHITECTURE

BACK-ILLUMINATED
STACKED SENSOR

Traditional sensors place the wiring layer between the light-sensitive photodiodes and the lens. BSI flips this arrangement — wiring goes behind, maximising the light-gathering surface of each pixel.

The stacked design adds a dedicated memory layer beneath the sensor, enabling reads at 60× the speed of conventional sensors.
