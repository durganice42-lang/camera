CAMERA TECHNOLOGY

PRECISION ENGINEERED

Six pillars of optical performance that define the cinematic camera experience.

High Resolution

61-megapixel full-frame BSI-CMOS sensor delivering extraordinary detail in every shot, from sweeping landscapes to intimate portraits.

01

Optical Zoom

10× optical zoom range with constant maximum aperture throughout. Reach your subject without compromise on light or clarity.

02

Precision Focus

693-point phase-detection AF with real-time eye and subject tracking. Lock onto your subject in 0.03 seconds with unerring accuracy.

03

Image Stabilization

7-stop in-body 5-axis stabilization eliminates camera shake. Combine with lens-based OIS for up to 8 stops of total compensation.

04

Cinematic Depth

S-Log3 and S-Cinetone colour profiles deliver Hollywood-grade dynamic range. 15+ stops of latitude for extraordinary post-production flexibility.

05

Low Light Performance

Expanded ISO range up to 409,600. Advanced noise reduction preserves fine texture in darkness. See in conditions the naked eye cannot.
